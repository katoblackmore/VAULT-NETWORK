Owning a Vault

A sealed Vault is a numbered record in your own wallet whose contents have not been released. Nothing expires and nothing opens on its own — the seal holds until you choose to unlock it.

In a wallet you control. The Network mints each Vault directly into the buyer's self-hosted wallet and never takes custody of it, or of the funds used to buy it.

Yes, and many Holders do. A sealed Vault can sit untouched indefinitely, or move to another Holder on an independent marketplace with the seal and its register number intact.

Buying and unlocking

A defined, stated number of Vaults at the tiers your amount covers. Nothing at the purchase step is chance-based, and the order is confirmed before payment is taken.

The unlock is written to the chain and cannot be reversed. The partner platform reads that record and releases what it holds against it. A Vault that has been unlocked does not return to circulation.
