Register

The Register

Every Vault is entered into the register on issue and carries its own number. Nothing repeats, and nothing carries a batch price. Partner platforms build the worlds where Vaults are honoured, and what a Vault opens onto is set by the world that accepts it.

Entries 001–004 are open for issue.

Each remains sealed until the Holder who owns it decides otherwise.

Register entry 001 Tier I · Common · Seal intact

Steel Vault

$10 per vault

Stamped, serialised, built in volume. Steel housings account for most of what the archive still holds, and most Holders begin here.

ConstructionRolled steel, single-wall
IssueProduction line
Held insideDry cells, plate stock, service-level tags.
StatusSealed, contents released only on unlock
Reward pool, set by the world it opens into
  • Charge
    Dry Cell

    Sealed power unit. Enough for routine operations and light systems.

  • Stock
    Plate Offcut

    Standard-grade material left from production runs.

  • Clearance

    Opens unrestricted areas and standard containers.

Register entry 002 Tier II · Uncommon · Seal intact

Obsidian Vault

Reinforced composite housing, issued to systems that were not permitted to fail. Deeper protocols, shorter production runs.

ConstructionLayered composite
IssueLimited run

StatusSealed, contents released only on unlock
